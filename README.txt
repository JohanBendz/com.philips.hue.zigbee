Make light part of the way your home lives. Let Philips Hue set the mood, welcome you home, respond to movement and bring everyday routines to life — all from Homey.

Connect supported Hue lights and accessories directly to Homey and bring them together with the rest of your smart home. No Hue Bridge required.

Update firmware on selected Hue lights and accessories through Homey. Firmware updates require a supported Homey platform running 13.2.0 or newer and Homey Mobile App 9.10.0 or newer. Availability depends on the exact device model and installed firmware; follow any wake instructions shown for battery devices.
